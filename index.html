Power-system model

The regional load is represented by maximum demand Pmax and load factor. The annual load-duration curve is approximated as a straight line. Plant economics determine which unit is economical for continuous operation and which should serve the higher-demand hours.

For a genuine base/peak trade-off, the base plant has the higher annual fixed cost but lower running cost; the peak plant has lower fixed cost but higher running cost.
